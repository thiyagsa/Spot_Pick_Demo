Trade Center Business Platform

Development Plan — 2 Main Stages

01 First priority
MVP Basic  
  Build the essential business flow first.
  FastAPI + PostgreSQL
  Modular Monolith architecture
  Core database schema and migrations
  Basic authentication and role permissions
  Product catalog and pricing
  One initial sales channel and essential order workflow
  Basic inventory and stock movements
  Basic billing, payment recording and audit trail
  Automated tests for critical business rules
Goal: Demonstrate one complete, reliable business workflow before adding advanced features.

02 Second priority
MVP to Production
  Harden, expand and prepare for real operations.
  Complete online sales and Trade Center POS integration
  Offline POS synchronization and conflict handling
  Multi-store and village-level inventory
  Procurement, stock transfers and returns
  Production-grade GST invoicing and credit notes
  Payment gateway integration, refunds and reconciliation
  Stronger security, auditability and access controls
  CI/CD, staging, production deployment and rollback
  Backups, recovery drills, monitoring and alerting
  Performance, concurrency and security testing
Goal: Safely operate the business with reliable transactions, recovery procedures and operational visibility.
